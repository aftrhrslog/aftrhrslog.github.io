{{- /* Every link written in markdown passes through here. A link that leaves the site opens in a new tab; one that stays on it does not, because taking over the reader's tab is only rude when they are being sent somewhere else. "Leaves the site" is decided by the destination actually starting with http:// or https:// and not with our own baseURL, so a full https://aftrhrslog.github.io/notes/ link is still treated as internal. Relative links, anchors, and mailto: are left alone. rel="noopener" stops the opened page reaching back through window.opener; noreferrer also withholds the referring URL. */ -}} {{- $d := .Destination -}} {{- $abs := or (hasPrefix $d "http://") (hasPrefix $d "https://") -}} {{- $mine := hasPrefix $d (strings.TrimSuffix "/" site.BaseURL) -}} {{- $out := and $abs (not $mine) -}} {{ .Text | safeHTML }}
