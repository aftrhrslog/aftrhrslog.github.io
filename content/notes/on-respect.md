Dear my son,

When people applaud you, accept their praise graciously. Take pride in what you’ve earned, but keep it in perspective. Let your sense of worth come from within rather than from other people’s approval. People sometimes compliment you simply because it’s polite, so don’t mistake courtesy for genuine respect.

If you find yourself in a position of power someday, stay grounded. The higher you rise, the harder it may be for people to be honest with you. A smile or a compliment doesn’t always mean that someone is comfortable with the way you’ve treated them.

Remember, too, that what seems like a small request to you may mean hours of unnecessary work for others who don’t feel free to say no. Before asking something of others, consider whether it is truly necessary and worth their time and effort. Know the limits of your own expertise, listen to those who know better, and don’t turn your personal interests or opinions into obligations for everyone else. How you value other people’s time, effort, and judgment will say far more about you than your position ever will.

Above all, stay humble and treat people well. Let respect come naturally rather than trying too hard to earn it or expecting others to prove it to you. Value people’s time and give them space. Let friendships grow naturally, and if they don’t, let things be. Good relationships don’t need to be forced. Sometimes the best way to get along is simply to be comfortable on our own and not ask too much of one another.
