---
title: "Two kinds of lists"
date: 2026-04-28
tags: ["Misc"]
description: "Lists that reduce anxiety and lists that produce it, and how to tell which one you are writing."
---

Some lists exist so that you can stop holding things in your head. Others exist
so that you can look at how much there is. These are not the same activity and
I have spent years running them together.

The test is simple: after writing it, do you feel lighter or heavier? If
heavier, you have written an inventory and called it a plan.
