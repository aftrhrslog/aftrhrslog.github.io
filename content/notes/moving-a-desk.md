For six months the desk faced a wall, because that is where the outlet was and
because I never questioned it. Then one Saturday I moved everything two metres
to the left, and the afternoon felt different in a way I could not immediately
account for.

## What actually changed

It was not the view, or not only the view. It was that my eyes had somewhere to
rest that was further away than an arm's length. Looking up now costs nothing
and returns something.

## What it cost

An extension lead and about forty minutes. The monitor glare I had been warned
about has not materialised, though I do close the blind around four in the
afternoon.

I keep thinking there is a general lesson here about how rarely we revisit the
first decision we made about a space. There probably is. I have not worked out
how to phrase it without sounding like an airport paperback.
