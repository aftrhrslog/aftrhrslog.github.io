title: Starting My Day Earlier Made It Feel Lighter
date: 2026-08-19T17:40:48+09:00
tags:
  - Thoughts
---
It’s only been a couple of days since I changed my daily routine. The change itself is very small: I simply started my day earlier.

My <mark>sleep schedule</mark> used to be pretty irregular. Sometimes I got up around 8 a.m., but other days I <mark>slept in</mark> until 10 or later. I started noticing a pattern. When I woke up late, I often <mark>felt rushed</mark> even though there was no real reason to hurry. I also <mark>felt strangely unsettled</mark>.

I think it’s mainly because I naturally divide my day into morning and afternoon, with lunch as a kind of <mark>dividing line</mark>. If I wake up at 10, there isn’t much morning left. That creates a strange pressure in my mind. When I <mark>feel pressured</mark>, it becomes harder to manage my mood, and when my mood <mark>dips</mark>, it can <mark>drag down</mark> my whole day.

So I decided to try getting up around 8 a.m. every day.

I haven’t changed much else. I still stretch on my yoga mat after waking up, wash my face, drink a glass of cold water, and have breakfast. I’m basically doing the same things, just earlier.

But surprisingly, I’ve been <mark>feeling more balanced</mark> since I made this small change.

Starting earlier gives me more <mark>breathing room</mark>. I can finish several things before lunch, so I don’t feel like I’m <mark>racing against the clock</mark>. More importantly, I feel like I have a little more <mark>mental space</mark>. I can keep some distance from things happening around me instead of reacting to every little thing.

That matters to me because I tend to <mark>read too much into</mark> little things, and one thought quickly leads to another. Before I know it, my mind <mark>feels overwhelmed</mark>.

I’ve been trying to change that. And somehow, simply waking up earlier seems to help. It makes it easier for me to let little things go instead of <mark>getting caught up in</mark> them.

Getting up early doesn’t magically solve everything, and sometimes getting out of bed at 8 is still difficult. But so far, it seems to help me <mark>stay more balanced</mark> throughout the day.
