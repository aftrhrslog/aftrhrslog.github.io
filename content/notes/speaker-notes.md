I bought it because it looked good on a shelf. That is the whole reason, and I
would like to be able to report that it was a mistake.

It was not. It turns out that having music start without opening an app, or
pairing anything, or waiting, changes how often music happens. The bar was
never sound quality. The bar was friction.

## Six weeks in

- Battery holds about eleven hours, which is more than the sticker claimed
- The physical dial is the best part and I did not consider it once before buying
- It is heavier than it looks in photographs

Would I buy it again at full price? Probably not. Am I glad it is here? Yes,
which is an uncomfortable pair of answers to hold at the same time.
