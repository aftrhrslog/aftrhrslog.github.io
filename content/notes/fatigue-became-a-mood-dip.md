title: When a Tired Body Troubles the Mind
date: 2026-08-24T13:00:00+09:00
tags:
  - Thoughts
---

Yesterday was Sunday, and my wife and I took a spontaneous trip to a nearby city. We had a great day, one of those days that <mark>feel full</mark> and satisfying without much planning.

Strangely, I didn’t feel particularly tired when we got home. But this morning, <mark>the fatigue seemed to arrive late</mark>. I woke up feeling drained, and along with that came <mark>a slight dip</mark> in my mood.

<mark>My first instinct was to</mark> ask *Why*. Once I start looking for a reason, my mind can easily find something to worry about and convince me that must be the reason. But maybe the order is sometimes simpler: the body feels tired first, and the mind starts creating stories to explain the feeling. Maybe I don’t need to find a reason at all. I might just be tired and need a little rest.

It’s Monday, and I had already planned plenty of things to do. Part of me wanted to stick to the plan exactly. I'd already <mark>pictured how the day is supposed to go</mark>. Should I take the day off and rest? Perhaps I don’t have to choose between pushing myself hard and doing absolutely nothing.

I can <mark>show up</mark>. Do a couple of important things. Work at a gentler pace. And if my body still feels tired afterward, I can rest.

Doing nothing for too long can actually make me feel empty. It's weird. But I need to accept myself as I am. Maybe I just need to <mark>take things a little easier today</mark>, not stop altogether.

So that’s my light conclusion for this Monday: listen to the body before <mark>believing every thought</mark>, solve the situation rather than every story around it, and <mark>leave a little room</mark> for the day to unfold differently from the plan.

Yesterday was a good day. Today can just be a softer one.

기분이 가라앉을 때, 우울함이라고 섣불리 이름붙이지 말자. 그것은 피곤함일 수도 있고, 아니면 살짝 다른 감정일 수도 있다. 실제로 무엇을 느끼는 것인지 가볍게 구분해보고, 꼭 원인을 찾아내려하거나 완벽하게 설명하려 하지는 말자. 실제로 우울감일지라도 내 삶에 지장을 주지 않는다면 그냥 잠깐 받아들이고 흘려 보내 주자.
