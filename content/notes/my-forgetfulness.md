When gathering my stuff before leaving the office, **I realized something was missing.**

**My medical bill**.

I needed to take the receipt after seeing a doctor. It seemed I hadn’t taken it. *What happened?* It was so embarrassing.

<mark>I thought back to what had happened.</mark> I had gone to a clinic earlier that morning. When I was paying at the kiosk, I was distracted by something I hadn’t finished. That’s when I must have forgotten to take the receipt. I didn’t even wait for the machine to print it before <mark> heading straight back to where I came from.</mark> I couldn’t believe I’d done that.

Then another thought hit me. *What if someone took a look at the receipt and it had <mark> sensitive personal information such as my resident registration number</mark> on it?* I’m very privacy-conscious.

I decided to stop overthinking it and just go back. Of course the receipt was gone. Someone might have taken it by mistake, or a staff member might have put it aside. Anyway, I still needed the receipt for <mark>insurance reimbursement.</mark> A staff member <mark>at the billing desk</mark> reissued my receipt. I looked over it carefully. Except for my patient ID number and name, there wasn’t any particularly sensitive personal information on it.

I was pretty embarrassed at first and a bit worried, but I <mark>laughed it off.</mark> Anyway, I need to pay more attention.
