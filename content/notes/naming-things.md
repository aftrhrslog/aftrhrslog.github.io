---
title: "On naming things badly and then living with it"
date: 2026-05-19
tags: ["Thoughts"]
description: "A folder called 'new2' that is four years old."
---

There is a folder on my drive called `new2`. It is four years old and contains
work I still refer to monthly. Renaming it would break perhaps a dozen links,
which is not a real obstacle, and yet.

The name is now doing something a good name would not: it carries history. I
know exactly what `new2` is, and the fact that it is badly named is part of how
I know.

I do not think this generalises. Most bad names are just bad.
