---
title: "The long way along the river"
date: 2026-06-30
tags: ["Leisure", "Misc"]
description: "An eleven-kilometre walk that should have taken two hours and took four."
---

The path on the north bank runs unbroken from the second bridge almost to the
edge of the hills. I had assumed it was fragmented, the way these things usually
are, and that assumption cost me a year of not walking it.

Start early. The stretch past the sports fields has no shade at all and by
eleven it is unpleasant. There is a bench roughly two thirds along, facing
back towards town, which is the single best place I have found to sit and do
nothing.
