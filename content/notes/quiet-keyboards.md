I did not buy anything. I want to record what I learned anyway, because in eight
months I will start this search again from zero unless I write it down.

## The measurement problem

Manufacturer decibel figures are close to meaningless. They are taken at
distances and angles nobody types at, and the number that matters to a person
in the next room is not loudness but how much of the sound sits in the two to
four kilohertz band, where human hearing is most sensitive.

## What actually reduces noise

Three things, roughly in order of effect:

1. Whether the switch bottoms out on rubber or plastic
2. What is under the plate — foam helps more than people admit
3. The desk itself, which is a drum

## Where I landed

Silent tactile switches on a gasket-mounted board, if I ever do this. The
marginal gain from anything more elaborate looked small compared to its cost.
