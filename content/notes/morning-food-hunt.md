It’s been three days since I started my early morning challenge. I’m still adjusting, but I’ve <mark>moved my wake-up time</mark> from around 10 a.m. to 7:30 a.m.

One thing I’ve noticed is that whenever I wake up earlier than usual, I feel like trying something unusual. I have more breathing room and mental space, and somehow that makes me want to do something a little special with the extra time.

This morning, on my way to the office, I stopped by a Burger King to try their new morning combo. I don’t usually eat much fast food, but I enjoy trying new <mark>menu items</mark> once in a while.

The new combo was decent, but it wasn’t the surprise I was hoping for. It reminded me a lot of the sandwiches from EggDrop, a Korean franchise I used to enjoy. It <mark>tastes a lot like</mark> EggDrop, especially because of the soft eggs, bread, and sauce. EggDrop isn’t very popular anymore, and it’s hard to find one in my area. In that sense, the Burger King menu could be an alternative. Still, I probably won’t <mark>go out of my way</mark> to get it again. Only a few Burger King <mark>locations</mark> sell it, and the ones near me don’t <mark>have no parking</mark>. There’s <mark>nowhere convenient to park</mark> nearby, so it’s too much of a hassle for something that was just okay.

But I don’t regret trying it. It was a small adventure, and that was really the point.

Looking back, most of my little early-morning adventures have involved <mark>food hunts</mark>. There <mark>aren’t that many</mark> things to do early in the morning, so places like McDonald’s become little destinations. They have breakfast menus <mark>you can only get in the morning</mark>.

Trying something unusual for breakfast also reminds me of traveling. It <mark>feels a little like</mark> having a hotel breakfast with eggs, bacon, and toast, or trying a local restaurant <mark>on a trip</mark>. For a moment, an ordinary morning feels slightly different.

The morning menus are just <mark>side quests</mark>. The real fun is having a little <mark>food hunt</mark> before starting my day. It adds some extra fun to my routine and makes the whole day <mark>feel a little lighter</mark>.
