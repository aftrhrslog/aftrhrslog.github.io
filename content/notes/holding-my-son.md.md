생후 3일째.

NICU에서 이틀째에는 CPAP도 떼고 이제는 OG tube도 빼서 숨을 잘 쉬고 분유도 잘 먹는데 혈당이 간헐적으로 낮아진다고 한다.

그래서 결국 내일 아내랑 같이 퇴원하지 못하고 이틀 더 경과관찰 해야 한다고.

그래도 이제는 몸에 달고 있는 것도 없고 붓기도 좀 빠져서 더 사람답게 보인다. 살도 보들보들하고 꿈틀꿈틀 꼼지락 귀엽다.

면회시간에 애기 안아보라고 하셔서 의자에 앉아 삼십분간 안고 있다 왔다.

너무 작고 연약해보이는 생명체라 조심스러웠다. 태어난 지 얼마 안 되었는데 그래도 묵직하니 생명의 무게가 느껴진다. 심장 박동도 느껴지고 숨쉴때마다 온몸으로 느껴진다.

작고 귀여운 친구가 생긴 기분이다. 잘 보살펴 줄테니 건강하게 자라렴.

Day 3.

<mark>On his second day</mark> in the NICU, he <mark>came off</mark> CPAP, and now the OG tube is out too. He’s breathing well and <mark>taking formula</mark> just fine, but apparently his <mark>blood sugar still drops</mark> from time to time.

So he won’t <mark>be discharged</mark> with my wife tomorrow after all. It looks like he’ll have to stay another two days so they can <mark>keep an eye on him</mark>.

Still, he's not <mark>hooked up to</mark> anything anymore, and <mark>the swelling has gone down</mark> a bit, so he's starting to look more like a little person. His skin is so soft, and it's cute watching him <mark>squirm and wiggle around</mark>.

During <mark>visiting hours</mark> they asked if I wanted to hold him, so I sat in a chair with him in my arms for about half an hour.

He was so tiny and fragile that <mark>I was almost afraid to move</mark>. He’d only <mark>been in the world for</mark> a few days, but holding him in my arms, I could really feel the weight of this tiny little life. I could feel his heartbeat and his little body <mark>rise and fall</mark> with every breath.

It felt like I had a tiny, adorable new buddy.

I’ll take good care of you, so just grow up healthy and strong.
