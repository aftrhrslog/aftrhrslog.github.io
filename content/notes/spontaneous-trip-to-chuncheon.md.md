Went to bed very late last night, felt sluggish when I woke up on my own around 7, slept in until 9, but still <mark>felt foggy</mark>.

Went outside for a walk to get some air and <mark>get my blood flowing</mark>, hoping I’d <mark>feel more refreshed</mark>. Took a cold shower and tried to take a nap.

Still couldn’t sleep and ended up going on a spontaneous trip to Chuncheon with my wife. It was my wife’s idea.

The Places We Visited

1. 통나무집 닭갈비 본점: After a quick search, we decided to visit this restaurant. It already had over 30K reviews <mark>on the Naver Map app</mark>. 닭갈비 (Dak-galbi, spicy stir-fried chicken) is a <mark>common dish</mark> found <mark>all over Korea</mark>, but Chuncheon is literally the city of 닭갈비. We had already visited a 닭갈비 restaurant in downtown Chuncheon before, but <mark>the food at this restaurant</mark> was somewhat different. The flavor wasn’t too strong, but everything was <mark>nicely balanced</mark>. The staff were kind and the service was great. <mark>I’d consider coming back</mark>.

2. 신북커피 (Sinbuk Coffee): I’m not a coffee lover. Not even a coffee person. My wife used to drink a lot of coffee, but not anymore because she is pregnant. Nevertheless, the unique exterior and interior of this cafe <mark>made us want to check it out</mark>. Its signature drink, 쑥미숫가루 (mugwort and mixed grain drink), was <mark>better than expected</mark>. We took a lot of photos in front of the old hanok cafe.

3. 소양강댐 (Soyang Dam): After we filled up, we turned our attention to the scenery and found this place nearby. We just wanted to <mark>walk off the meal</mark> there. We could walk across the dam, and there was a well-maintained <mark>walking path</mark>. <mark>The view looking down from</mark> the dam was quite magnificent. From the top, <mark>we realized just how</mark> high the dam was and <mark>couldn’t help wondering how</mark> they <mark>managed to</mark> build such a huge structure. We arrived late, so <mark>I wished I’d had more time</mark> to look around. There were a few walking paths we didn’t <mark>get a chance</mark> to explore.
